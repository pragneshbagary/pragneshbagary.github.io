go-thin

Composable Go framework of small, interface-driven libraries.

  • go-auth, go-paginate and go-events compose through interfaces and never depend on each other
  • go-auth: JWT access/refresh lifecycle with rotation and revocation, Argon2id hashing, RBAC, adapters for Gin, Echo and Fiber, and PostgreSQL, SQLite and in-memory storage
  • Test-first: 100% coverage on go-paginate, 96% on go-events, ~74% on the go-auth core
